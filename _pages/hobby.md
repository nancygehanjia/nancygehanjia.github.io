## Riichi Mahjong

I also enjoy playing Riichi Mahjong (AKA Japanese Mahjong) — the most complex type of mahjong, where players play more defensively because the penalty for dealing into another player's hand is high. Here is more info: [Riichi Wiki](https://riichi.wiki/Majsoul).
