My name is Gehan Jia, and I also go by Nancy. I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (expected Dec 2026), focusing on Formal Methods while exploring Game Development and creative technology. I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science from UIUC in May 2024, with a minor in Critical Film Production.

## What I'm Working On

**[SelfReflectiveRatings](https://claude.d1ef5vsv1cb198.amplifyapp.com/)** — A full-stack web application for personality self-assessments and multiplayer peer rating games, built in collaboration with UIUC Psychology Department research teams. Built with React, Supabase, and PostgreSQL, deployed on AWS Amplify.

**Poo's Voice Transformer** — A real-time voice effects desktop application with pitch shifting and audio effects (robot, autotune, echo, 8-bit). Built with Python, PyQt6, and SciPy.

## Experience

I have been a Teaching Assistant for **CS 124** (Intro to Computer Science) at UIUC since 2021, where I develop course content in Java and Kotlin, build automated grading systems, and support 1000+ students per semester. I also served as a Course Assistant for **CS 477** (Formal Software Development Methods).

My research experience includes work at the **Focal Lab** on optimizing pointer analysis frameworks using reinforcement learning, and at the **Illinois Geometry Lab** studying graph theory and the Burning Number Conjecture.
