## Overview

A 3D multiplayer RPG developed for **That Game Jam #1** by Team ^QAQ^. The game features puzzle-driven gameplay and is available as a free download on Windows and macOS, with a full multiplayer mode.

[Play on itch.io](https://poo-0331.itch.io/in-the-blue-multiplayer-available)
